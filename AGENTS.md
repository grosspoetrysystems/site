# AGENTS.md

This is the portfolio of Gross Poetry Systems. Work here is judged on brand, voice and craft.

## Voice

- Copy comes from the owner. Don't write taglines, descriptions or marketing prose; ask.
- GPS is the author. No person's name anywhere.

## The test for every decision

1. Would this look out of place in a 1972 records office? If it looks distinctly of 2026, reconsider.
2. Is the humor structural or decorative? Decorative humor is cut.
3. Does it earn its idiosyncrasy? The permission to be strange is bought by rigor. If the rigor isn't there, cut the strange first.
4. Would the original — Vignelli, Müller-Brockmann, the KPM art department — be honored by this, or embarrassed by it?

Subtract until restraint shows. Features that don't earn their keep are removed, not deferred.
